Profile

Who I am, in my own words.

I'm a Mobile Application Developer and Operations team member at Zippintella (Zippi), a ride-hailing startup in Hyderabad. On the development side, I work on our Flutter apps for Android and iOS, help debug Laravel services, job queues and database issues, and manage the GitHub repositories across our mobile codebases. On the operations side, I verify driver-partner documents during onboarding, including driving licences, Aadhaar, vehicle RC, and background checks.

I'm early in my developer journey, and most of my learning comes from real production problems: working with a cross-functional team to trace an issue from the app to the backend and the database until it's fixed. I also set up and maintain the Telegram ride-alert automation our operations team relies on.

I started out in digital marketing (SEO, analytics, WordPress, email campaigns), so I think about what happens after a feature ships. People have to find it, use it and trust it. Outside work, I'm a photographer, which trained my eye for detail and good UI.
